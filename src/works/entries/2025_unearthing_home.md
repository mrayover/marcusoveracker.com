*Words by* **Sharon K. McClaine**:<br><br>

“Sand thrashed cyan sea where I was born.<br>
Coast quaked while earth rocked my bones.<br>
Plates slipped, tectonic and tribal.<br>
I yearned for a home.<br>
Fate lured me to this fertile valley.<br>
Foothills like blue-backed whales flanked the ancient, inland sea.<br>
I stream like snowmelt to this alluvial ground.<br>
I am home.”<br>
